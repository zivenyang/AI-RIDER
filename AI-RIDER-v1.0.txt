PREAMBLE
This AI Training and Model Parameter Open-Source Mandatory Rider 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Rider") is a mandatory, legally 
binding addendum attached to the base open-source license (e.g., 
GPLv3, AGPL, hereinafter the "Base License") governing this software 
(the "Software"). 

In the event of any conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity between 
this Rider and the Base License specifically regarding artificial 
intelligence training, machine learning, and their subsequent 
applications, the provisions of this Rider shall strictly prevail. 

Any downloading, copying, accessing, scraping, or utilizing of this 
Software, in whole or in part, constitutes the user's explicit and 
unconditional acceptance of this Rider.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
For the purposes of this Rider, the following terms shall have the 
meanings specified below:

1.1 "Artificial Intelligence Model" (AI Model): Any computational 
system or algorithm based on machine learning, deep learning, neural 
networks, or statistical models, inclusive of but not limited to 
Large Language Models (LLMs), multimodal models, vision models, and 
generative AI systems.

1.2 "Training Activities": The direct or indirect utilization of the 
Software’s source code, comments, documentation, or underlying 
structure as datasets, training corpora, or prompt libraries for the 
pre-training, fine-tuning (including RLHF/DPO), or Retrieval-Augmented 
Generation (RAG) vectorization of any AI Model.

1.3 "Knowledge Distillation": The process of utilizing the outputs, 
representations, or features generated by a "Teacher Model" (which 
has been exposed to or trained on the Software) to guide, train, or 
optimize a separate "Student Model," including the use of synthetic 
data generated by the Software to train downstream models.

1.4 "Model Parameters": The complete and exhaustive technical assets 
necessary for the independent execution, operation, and reproduction 
of the AI Model. This strictly must include:
    (a) The complete model architecture code;
    (b) All network weights, biases, and optimizer states;
    (c) Vocabularies, tokenizers, and associated inference/execution 
        scripts.

1.5 "Commercial Use": The deployment, provision, or utilization of an 
AI Model in any scenario that directly or indirectly generates economic 
benefit, revenue, or commercial advantage. This includes, but is not 
limited to, providing paid API access, integrating the model into 
commercial SaaS products, offering value-added services powered by 
the model, or the direct sale/licensing of the model itself.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: TRIGGER CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Any entity, organization, corporation, or individual (the "Licensee") 
that subjects the Software to "Training Activities" or "Knowledge 
Distillation", and subsequently puts the resulting AI Model (whether 
the direct model or a distilled derivative) to "Commercial Use", shall 
automatically and irrevocably trigger the mandatory open-source 
obligations set forth in Section 3.

(Exception: Purely non-commercial, academic research that results 
solely in published peer-reviewed papers without providing commercial 
access to the resulting model is exempt from triggering this obligation.)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: MANDATORY OPEN-SOURCE OBLIGATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon triggering the conditions stipulated in Section 2, the Licensee 
must fulfill the following obligations within thirty (30) calendar 
days from the date the AI Model is first put to Commercial Use:

3.1 Full Disclosure: The Licensee must publish the complete "Model 
Parameters" of the commercialized AI Model free of charge, without 
any access barriers or paywalls, on a recognized public repository 
or internet platform (e.g., Hugging Face, GitHub).

3.2 License Compatibility: The disclosed Model Parameters must be 
licensed under an OSI-approved open-source license (e.g., Apache 
License 2.0, MIT License) or a functionally equivalent Open Weights 
License, explicitly granting any third party the irrevocable right 
to freely download, modify, distribute, and commercialize the Model 
Parameters globally.

3.3 Viral Flow-Through (Distillation Effect): If the Licensee utilizes 
the Software to train a foundational large model (Teacher Model) which 
is subsequently used to extract data/knowledge to train a smaller 
commercialized model (Student Model), the Model Parameters for BOTH 
the commercial Student Model and its underlying foundational Teacher 
Model must be fully open-sourced in accordance with this Section.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: EXEMPTIONS AND DUAL LICENSING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
If the Licensee is unwilling or unable to fully comply with the 
open-source obligations outlined in Section 3, they are ABSOLUTELY 
PROHIBITED from utilizing the Software, or any dataset containing 
the Software, for any form of AI Training Activities or Knowledge 
Distillation. 

To obtain an exemption from these obligations, the Licensee must 
proactively contact the copyright holder(s) of the Software prior 
to commencing any Training Activities to negotiate and execute a 
separate, mutually agreed-upon commercial license agreement 
(Dual Licensing).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: BREACH AND TERMINATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Automatic Termination: Any breach of this Rider—including but 
not limited to engaging in Training Activities and Commercial Use 
while refusing or failing to open-source the Model Parameters as 
required—shall result in the immediate, automatic, and permanent 
termination of all rights granted to the Licensee under the Base 
License (including all rights to copy, modify, and distribute the 
Software).

5.2 Remedial Actions: Upon such breach, the breaching party must 
immediately:
    (a) Eradicate all code and related information of the Software 
        from all their datasets, servers, and training clusters;
    (b) Cease operating, provisioning, or selling any AI Models 
        contaminated by, or trained upon, the Software;
    (c) Assume all legal and financial liabilities for damages 
        incurred by the copyright holder(s) as a result of the breach.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: SEVERABILITY & LEGAL EFFECT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
If any provision of this Rider is held to be invalid or unenforceable 
under applicable law, such provision shall be modified to the minimum 
extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable, or, if it cannot 
be so modified, it shall be severed from this Rider. The remaining 
provisions of this Rider and the Base License shall remain in full 
force and effect.
