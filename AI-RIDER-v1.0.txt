This AI Training and Model Parameter Open-Source Mandatory Rider 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Rider") is a mandatory, legally 
binding addendum attached to the base open-source license (e.g., 
GPLv3, AGPL, hereinafter the "Base License") governing this software 
(the "Software"). 

In the event of any conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity between
this Rider and the Base License specifically regarding artificial
intelligence, machine learning, automated data processing, retrieval-

this Rider shall strictly prevail.

Any downloading, copying, accessing, scraping, or utilizing of this

unconditional acceptance of this Rider.

SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
For the purposes of this Rider, the following terms shall have the 
meanings specified below:

1.1 "Artificial Intelligence Model" (AI Model): Any computational
system, algorithm, or model that is capable of learning from data,
generating predictions, producing outputs, or performing tasks that
would otherwise require human intelligence, regardless of the
specific architecture, methodology, or paradigm employed. This
includes, but is not limited to, systems based on machine learning,
deep learning, neural networks, statistical models, symbolic AI,
neuro-symbolic hybrids, or any successor, derivative, or functionally
equivalent technology — encompassing Large Language Models (LLMs),
multimodal models, vision models, generative AI systems, and any
future systems that achieve comparable or analogous functionality
through any means.

1.2 "Training Activities": Any process, method, or technique — whether
currently known or developed in the future — by which the Software’s
source code, comments, documentation, structure, logic, or any
information derived therefrom is used, directly or indirectly, to
create, build, improve, adapt, optimize, or influence an AI Model’s
internal parameters, behavior, capabilities, or outputs. This
includes, but is not limited to, pre-training, fine-tuning,
reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), direct preference
optimization (DPO), continual learning, online learning, curriculum
learning, self-supervised learning, semi-supervised learning,
federated learning, meta-learning, transfer learning, synthetic data
generation used for downstream training, and any functionally
equivalent or successor methodology that achieves a comparable effect
on any AI Model, regardless of the terminology used to describe it.
Note: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and analogous retrieval-
based mechanisms that reference the Software’s content at inference
time without modifying the AI Model’s internal parameters do not
constitute a "Training Activity" under this Rider; however, commercial
use of such mechanisms involving this Software is subject to the
attribution and notice obligations set forth in Section 3A.

1.3 "Knowledge Distillation": Any process — whether currently known
or developed in the future — by which the knowledge, capabilities,
behavior patterns, or representations of one AI Model (the "Source
Model") that has been exposed to or trained on the Software are
transferred, extracted, compressed, or otherwise conveyed to a
separate AI Model (the "Derived Model"). This includes, but is not
limited to, traditional teacher-student distillation, self-
distillation, mutual learning, feature alignment, logit matching,
attention transfer, data-free distillation, the generation and use
of synthetic data produced by the Source Model to train the Derived
Model, and any functionally equivalent or successor methodology,
regardless of the terminology used to describe it.

1.4 "Model Parameters": The complete and exhaustive set of technical
assets necessary for the independent execution, operation, and full
reproduction of the AI Model. This includes, but is not limited to:
    (a) The complete model architecture definition and source code;
    (b) All learned parameters in any form — including but not limited
        to weights, biases, embeddings, adapter parameters (e.g.,
        LoRA matrices), optimizer states, normalization statistics,
        state-space parameters, and any other internal numerical
        values that have been created or modified through Training
        Activities;
    (c) All preprocessing and postprocessing components required for
        inference — including but not limited to vocabularies,
        tokenizers, feature extractors, data processors, and
        associated inference or execution scripts;
    (d) Any configuration files, hyperparameter records, or metadata
        necessary to reproduce the model's behavior.

1.5 "Commercial Use": The deployment, provision, or utilization of an
AI Model or retrieval system in any scenario that directly or
indirectly generates economic benefit, revenue, or commercial
advantage for any entity. This includes, but is not limited to,
providing paid or subscription-based API access, integrating the
model or system into commercial products or services (including SaaS,
PaaS, embedded systems, and on-premise solutions), offering value-
added services powered by the model or system, the direct sale,
licensing, or sublicensing of the model itself, using the model or
system to reduce operational costs in a revenue-generating business,
and any other form of economic exploitation — regardless of whether
the end user is charged directly, and regardless of the terminology
used to describe the commercial arrangement.

1.6 "Retrieval Use": Any process — whether currently known or
developed in the future — by which the Software's source code,
comments, documentation, structure, or any information derived
therefrom is indexed, vectorized, embedded, stored, cached, or
otherwise organized into any retrieval system (including but not
limited to knowledge bases, vector databases, graph databases,
search indices, caching layers, or any analogous storage or indexing
mechanism), for the purpose of retrieval, reference, presentation,
or delivery at inference time or query time, via Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) or any functionally equivalent or successor
retrieval-based mechanism, in any scenario that constitutes
"Commercial Use" as defined in Section 1.5.

SECTION 2: TRIGGER CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Training and Distillation: Any entity, organization, corporation,
or individual (the "Licensee") that subjects the Software to "Training
Activities" or "Knowledge Distillation", and subsequently puts the
resulting AI Model (whether the direct model or a distilled derivative)
to "Commercial Use", shall automatically and irrevocably trigger the
mandatory open-source obligations set forth in Section 3.

2.2 Retrieval Use: Any entity, organization, corporation, or
individual (the "Licensee") that engages in "Retrieval Use" of the
Software shall automatically trigger the attribution and notice
obligations set forth in Section 3A.

(Exception: Purely non-commercial, academic research that results
solely in published peer-reviewed papers without providing commercial
access to the resulting model or retrieval system is exempt from
triggering the obligations in this Section.)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: MANDATORY OPEN-SOURCE OBLIGATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon triggering the conditions stipulated in Section 2.1, the Licensee
must fulfill the following obligations within thirty (30) calendar
days from the date the AI Model is first put to Commercial Use:

3.1 Full Disclosure: The Licensee must publish the complete "Model 
Parameters" of the commercialized AI Model free of charge, without 
any access barriers or paywalls, on a recognized public repository 
or internet platform (e.g., Hugging Face, GitHub).

3.2 License Compatibility: The disclosed Model Parameters must be 
licensed under an OSI-approved open-source license (e.g., Apache 
License 2.0, MIT License) or a functionally equivalent Open Weights 
License, explicitly granting any third party the irrevocable right 
to freely download, modify, distribute, and commercialize the Model 
Parameters globally.

3.3 Viral Flow-Through (Distillation Effect): If the Licensee utilizes
the Software to train an AI Model (the "Source Model") which is
subsequently used — through Knowledge Distillation or any other
means — to create, improve, or influence a separate commercialized
AI Model (the "Derived Model"), the Model Parameters for BOTH the
commercial Derived Model and its underlying Source Model must be
fully open-sourced in accordance with this Section. This obligation
applies regardless of the number of intermediate models in the
chain and regardless of the specific technique used to transfer
knowledge between models.

SECTION 3A: RETRIEVAL USE — ATTRIBUTION AND NOTICE OBLIGATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Any entity, organization, corporation, or individual (the "Licensee")
that engages in "Retrieval Use" of the Software shall comply with the
following attribution and notice obligations:

3A.1 Attribution: When the retrieval system generates, presents, or
delivers any output that is derived from, references, or incorporates
content retrieved from the Software, the Licensee must provide clear
and prominent attribution to the original project, including at
minimum:
    (a) The name of the Software and/or its repository;
    (b) A direct URL or hyperlink to the Software's canonical source
        repository;
    (c) The name of the copyright holder(s).

3A.2 License Notice: Alongside the attribution required in 3A.1, the
Licensee must clearly inform the end user (the "Code Consumer") that
the retrieved content is governed by AI-RIDER. This notice must
include:
    (a) An explicit statement that the original source code is
        protected under AI-RIDER;
    (b) A direct URL or hyperlink to the full text of this Rider
        (the Canonical URL specified in the header of this document);
    (c) A statement informing the Code Consumer that any use of the
        retrieved code for AI Training Activities or Knowledge
        Distillation is subject to the obligations of this Rider.

3A.3 Presentation Requirements: The attribution and license notice
required by 3A.1 and 3A.2 must be:
    (a) Displayed in a manner reasonably visible and accessible to
        the Code Consumer at the point of content delivery (e.g.,
        inline with the generated output, in a clearly associated
        metadata panel, or in an immediately accessible tooltip or
        footnote);
    (b) Not hidden, obscured, or buried in terms of service or
        documentation that the Code Consumer is unlikely to read
        during normal use.

3A.4 Scope: This Section applies regardless of whether the Retrieval
Use modifies the AI Model's weights. The obligations herein are
independent of, and in addition to, the obligations set forth in
Section 3.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Distillation, or Retrieval Use.

To obtain an exemption from these obligations, the Licensee must
proactively contact the copyright holder(s) of the Software prior
to commencing any Training Activities or Retrieval Use to negotiate
and execute a separate, mutually agreed-upon commercial license
agreement (Dual Licensing).

SECTION 5: BREACH AND TERMINATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Automatic Termination: Any breach of this Rider—including but
not limited to engaging in Training Activities and Commercial Use
while refusing or failing to open-source the Model Parameters as
required, or engaging in Retrieval Use while refusing or failing to
provide the attribution and notice required by Section 3A—shall
result in the immediate, automatic, and permanent termination of all
rights granted to the Licensee under the Base License (including all
rights to copy, modify, and distribute the Software).

5.2 Remedial Actions: Upon such breach, the breaching party must
immediately:
    (a) Eradicate all code and related information of the Software
        from all their datasets, retrieval systems, knowledge bases,
        servers, training clusters, and any other storage or
        processing infrastructure;
    (b) Cease operating, provisioning, or selling any AI Models or
        retrieval-based services that have been contaminated by,
        trained upon, or that reference the Software;
    (c) Assume all legal and financial liabilities for damages
        incurred by the copyright holder(s) as a result of the breach.
